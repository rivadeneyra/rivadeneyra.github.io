Economist working on money, payments and technology

Francisco Rivadeneyra

I study money and payments, and how new technology changes them. I am Senior Director, Payments Policy and Research at the Bank of Canada.

My research covers payment system design, digital currencies, and the use of AI and quantum computing in finance and central banking. I hold a PhD in economics from the University of Chicago.

About

My early work at the Bank of Canada was on the Government of Canada's debt management and foreign exchange reserves, including the models the Bank and the Department of Finance use to weigh cost against risk.

I later led the Bank's CBDC and fintech policy and research: a research programme on digital currencies, the 2023 Bank of Canada–Riksbank conference on the economics of CBDC, and experiments applying machine learning and quantum computing to payment system data.

Today I lead the policy function of the Payments and Regulatory Oversight department, which anticipates emerging issues in payments and grounds the Bank's strategic choices in economic research and technical experimentation.

Short bio for event organisers

Francisco Rivadeneyra is Senior Director, Payments Policy and Research at the Bank of Canada, where he leads the policy function of the Payments and Regulatory Oversight department. His work covers payment system design, digital money, and the use of AI and quantum computing in finance. He previously led the Bank's CBDC and fintech policy and research, and earlier worked on Government of Canada debt and reserves management. He holds a PhD in economics from the University of Chicago.
